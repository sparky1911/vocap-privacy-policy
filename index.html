Vocap (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a vocabulary memory quiz application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Vocap mobile application (“App”).

1. Information stored on your device

Vocap stores quiz-related information, such as your answers and quiz progress, locally on your device so the App can provide its learning features. Based on the App’s current design, this information is not stored in a Vocap account or on a Vocap server.

Local quiz information may be lost if you uninstall the App, clear its app data, or reset your device. The App does not require you to create an account or sign in.

2. Audio

Vocap does not record audio or process speech.

3. Data transmission and sharing

Vocap is designed to keep quiz answers and progress on your device rather than send them to a Vocap server. Developer: verify that the App and its included SDKs do not send analytics, crash reports, device identifiers, or other information to third parties before publishing this statement as final.

4. Retention and deletion

Quiz-related information is stored locally by the App. It may be deleted when you uninstall the App, clear its storage, or reset your device. The App does not provide account-based cloud storage for quiz progress.

5. Permissions

Vocap does not need microphone access for audio recording or speech processing. Any permissions requested by the App should be limited to those needed for its actual features.

6. Children’s privacy

The App’s intended audience and its practices regarding children’s information should be accurately declared in Google Play Console. Any additional requirements applicable to the selected audience must be met.

7. Security

Quiz-related information is stored in the App’s local data on your device. Protecting access to your device helps protect that information. No method of storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or our practices change. We will update the “Last updated” date when we publish a revised policy.
